# Code of Conduct

## Our Team

**TERMINAL LARPERS** is a four-person team competing in AppBuildersPH Hackathon 2026. This document sets the expectations for how we work together during the build period and beyond.

---

## Our Commitment

We are committed to keeping our collaboration respectful, focused, and productive. Every team member — regardless of their role or experience level — deserves to contribute without fear of ridicule, dismissal, or hostility.

---

## Expected Behavior

- **Communicate openly.** Surface blockers, questions, or concerns early so the team can adapt.
- **Respect each other's time.** The build window is short. Keep discussions focused and decisions decisive.
- **Give and receive feedback constructively.** Critique the code, not the person. When reviewing a pull request, be specific and solution-oriented.
- **Take ownership.** If you picked up a task, see it through or hand it off explicitly — don't leave it in limbo.
- **Share context.** Document decisions and findings so no one is left guessing. Leave clear commit messages and PR descriptions.
- **Acknowledge contributions.** Credit teammates for their work in code comments, commit messages, and the submission disclosure.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Dismissing or mocking a teammate's idea or implementation.
- Making changes to another member's branch without consent.
- Submitting work that misrepresents who wrote it.
- Including any content in the codebase that is discriminatory, offensive, or harmful.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces: this repository, group chats, the build venue, and Demo Day at SM Makati.

---

## Enforcement

Issues should be raised directly with the team. If a behavior is disruptive to the build or the team's wellbeing, any member may call it out. The goal is resolution, not punishment — we have a deadline to hit together.
