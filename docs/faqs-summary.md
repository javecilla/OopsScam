## **1\. Quick Cheat Sheet & Timeline**

* **Build Period (Remote):** October 9, 1:00 PM hanggang October 10, 10:00 AM.  
* **Hard Deadline:** **October 10, 2026, 10:00 AM sharp** sa Cerebral Valley event page. Walang extensions.  
* **Code Freeze:** Public GitHub repo dapat by 10:00 AM.  
* **Demo Day Arrival:** At least 1 member must be on-site sa Cyberzone, SM Makati by **12:00 PM (Oct 10\)**.  
* **Finalists Announcement:** 1:00 PM (Oct 10\) on-site (10 to 15 teams).

## **2\. Submission Deliverables Checklist**

Siguraduhing kumpleto ang mga ito bago mag-10:00 AM bukas:

1. **Cerebral Valley Form:** 1 submission lang per team. Dito ilalagay ang final team name, official members, at exact roles/contributions.  
2. **Public GitHub Repo:**  
   * Public by 10:00 AM.  
   * Hindi required ang live deployment, pero **mandatory** ang clear setup/recreation instructions sa `README.md` para ma-run ng judges.  
3. **Demo Video (\~1 minute):** Mabilisang product walkthrough.  
4. **Social Post:** I-post ang demo video sa X (Twitter) o LinkedIn. I-tag ang **Devin / Cognition** at gamitin ang hashtag `#AppBuildersPH`.  
5. **Tool Disclosure:** Ilista lahat ng AI tools na ginamit (Devin, Cursor, Copilot, LLM APIs, etc.).

## **3\. Demo & Pitch Mechanics**

* **Pitch Format:** 5 minutes live pitch \+ working demo, plus 3 minutes technical Q\&A. Focus agad sa working product over slides.  
* **Judges Defense (10 Judges):** Technical evaluation ito. Expect code reviews, questions sa actual architecture, model choices, limitations, at patunay na ginawa ito from scratch within the hackathon period.  
* **On-site Requirements:** Walang remote pitching. Bring your own laptop, chargers, and display adapters (HDMI and USB-C provided sa stage).

## **4\. Key FAQs (Consolidated)**

**Registration & Roster**

* **Pwede pa ba magdagdag ng unregistered teammate?** Hindi na. Disqualification ang paglista ng member na wala sa official registry.  
* **Naiwan ang teammate ko, pwede pa ba ako tumuloy?** Oo, pwede kang mag-submit as solo builder or makipag-partner sa ibang registered soloists sa Telegram.  
* **Paano palitan ang team name o groupings?** Sa mismong final project submission form na ito aayusin.

**Building Rules**

* **Required ba pumunta sa overnight venue?** Hindi. Fully remote ang hackathon. Ang space sa White Cloak ay optional at fully booked na.  
* **Allowed ba ang AI tools?** Yes, 100% allowed ang AI coding agents (tulad ng Devin) basta i-declare during submission.  
* **Pwede ba humingi ng tulong sa labas?** Bawal ang external collaborators na hindi registered sa event.

**Demo Day & Perks**

* **Paano kung di kami umabot sa finals?** Worth it pa rin pumunta. May raffle draws, networking, sandwich/coffee, at laban para sa People's Choice Award via QR code voting.  
* **Sino ang may-ari ng IP/code?** Sa team niyo 100% ang ownership ng code at project.  
* **Saan makukuha ang certificates?** Direct download sa official participant list sa `[appbuildersph.com/hackathon](https://appbuildersph.com/hackathon)`.
