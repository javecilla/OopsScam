1. PRE-RECORDING SETUP & PHONE PREPARATION CHECKLIST
================================================================================

[ ] DEVICE PREPARATION:
    - Android phone running the OopsScam release APK (bundled Qwen3 0.6B GGUF model).
    - AIRPLANE MODE: Turn ON (Wi-Fi OFF, Mobile Data OFF). Keep Bluetooth OFF.
    - BRIGHTNESS: Set to ~80% manual brightness to avoid camera flicker/glare.
    - NOTIFICATIONS: Enable "Do Not Disturb" so no stray popups interrupt recording.
    - RECORDING SETUP: Either screen record on-device (1080p, 60fps) OR record 
      hands-on-phone with an external camera/tripod (showing phone physically 
      isolated in airplane mode creates maximum trust).

[ ] ASSET PRE-LOADING:
    - Clipboard: Have Sample 1 (Fake GCash smishing) already copied or in a memo pad.
    - Gallery: Have 1 screenshot of a fake bank/ayuda alert ready in the Photos gallery.
    - Physical Prop: Have a printed or screen-displayed QR code (phishing link or QR Ph).
    - App State: Clear previous sessions so Home Screen opens fresh.
    - History: Optionally have 1-2 previous scans in Scan History to show persistence.
